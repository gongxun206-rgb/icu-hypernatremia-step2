- Common T0/T1 and `[T0,T1)` direct-fluid exposure window.
- Common ICU-only outcome window: `[T1,min(ICU discharge, ICU admission+7 d, death))`.
- Amsterdam sodium item 9924; items 9555 and 10284 were not pooled.
- Amsterdam pre-T0 hypertonic screen: 2.9% NaCl item 10739.
- Amsterdam direct fluids: NaCl 7293, LR 7316, D5W 7257.
- MIMIC direct fluids retain the frozen Step 2 definitions.
- H1 adjustment: age group, sex, last pre-T0 sodium, and two non-focal direct-fluid co-exposure indicators.
- Amsterdam complete-case handling excluded 51 outcome-observed admissions with undocumented sex.
- Carrier exposure was not externally replicated.
- Models remained database-specific; no pooled estimate or heterogeneity meta-analysis was performed.
