# Frozen protocol lock

- T0: ICU admission +24 h.
- T1: T0 +12 h.
- Primary exposure window: `[T0,T1)`; 6 h and 24 h were prespecified structural sensitivities.
- MIMIC primary outcome window: `[T1,min(ICU admission+7 d, hospital discharge, death))`.
- Harmonized outcome window: `[T1,min(ICU discharge, ICU admission+7 d, death))`.
- Outcome: first recorded serum/plasma sodium >=151 mmol/L.
- No eligible post-T1 sodium: outcome unascertained, never non-event.
- Primary exposures: documented direct 0.9% NaCl per 500 mL, LR per 500 mL, and D5W per 250 mL.
- Direct fluids and medication carriers are separate constructs.
- ACTIVE interface with no qualifying record may represent observed zero; SILENT remains unknown.
- No pooled estimate, heterogeneity meta-analysis, threshold search, or causal treatment-effect claim.
